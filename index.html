No popups. No server uploads. Just instant, private text formatting.

Whether you're fixing messy capitalization copied from a PDF, formatting database identifiers in camelCase, or checking the readability score of an article, ConvertsText runs everything locally on your device in real time.

Why use ConvertsText?

  • Zero Data Uploads: Your text never leaves your browser memory. Ideal for confidential documents, sensitive code, passwords, and private notes.
  • Built for Speed & Keyboard Flow: Press Ctrl+K to search tools instantly. Use Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Y to undo and redo changes without page reloads.
  • Linguistic Accuracy: Our Title Case converter follows AP and Chicago editorial stylebooks, keeping minor words (a, an, the, in, on, at, of) in lowercase unless they start a sentence.

Available Conversion Modes

  • Sentence case: Capitalizes the first letter of each sentence while preserving standalone capital pronouns like 'I'.
  • Title Case: Headlines and title formatting adhering to AP and Chicago editorial rules.
  • UPPER CASE: Transforms all characters into capitals for high-visibility alerts or acronyms.
  • lower case: Converts all characters to lowercase for URL slugs and database normalization.
  • Developer Cases: camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case, kebab-case, and CONSTANT_CASE with intelligent word boundary tokenization.
  • Small Caps & Stylized Fonts: Unicode aesthetic characters (ꜱᴍᴀʟʟ ᴄᴀᴘꜱ, ⓑⓤⓑⓑⓛⓔ, 𝔊𝔬𝔱𝔥𝔦𝔠) for social media bios and Discord handles.
  • Ciphers & Binary: 8-bit ASCII binary bytes, hexadecimal, Base64 strings, and Morse code with an integrated audio synthesizer.
