🔒 Transparency & User Safety

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 16, 2026. Your privacy and data security are our highest priority.

🛡️ Summary of Our Core Privacy Pledge:

All text conversion, case manipulation, readability analysis, and cryptographic password generation tools run 100% locally in your web browser using JavaScript. Your input text is never uploaded, saved, transmitted, or logged on our servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Unlike traditional online converters that upload your text to a backend API for processing, ConvertsText operates strictly client-side. When you paste or type sensitive documents, source code, or passwords into our tools, that data remains strictly inside your device's memory.

2. Information We Automatically Collect (Log Files & Analytics)

Like most standard websites, ConvertsText may collect non-personally identifiable information through standard server logs and web analytics. This may include:

  • Browser type and version (e.g. Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
  • Operating system (e.g. Windows, macOS, Android, iOS)
  • Referring website URLs and timestamps of visits
  • General aggregated geographic region (e.g. Country or City level)
  • Pages viewed and total interaction time to improve website speed and performance

3. Cookies and Web Beacons

ConvertsText uses local storage and cookies for functional preferences, such as remembering your choice of Dark Mode or Light Mode theme and user interface settings. You can manage or disable cookies at any time through your browser settings.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Policies

We may display third-party advertisements via Google AdSense and its certified advertising partners to support the free operation of this website:

  • Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on their visit to this and other websites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting the Google Ads Settings page.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (Do Not Sell My Personal Information)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request disclosure of personal data collected, request deletion, and request that a business not sell their personal data. ConvertsText does not sell personal data to any third party.

6. GDPR Data Protection Rights

For users within the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data we hold. Since our tools process text purely in your local browser, no user text is collected or stored.

7. Children's Information

ConvertsText does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided personal information on our website, please contact us immediately so we can promptly remove such records.

8. Contact Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions, suggestions, or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us via our Contact Page or by email at privacy@convertstext.com.
