How do Fancy Unicode Fonts Work?

Unlike standard computer fonts (like Arial or Times New Roman) which require special software installed on the reader's device, Unicode font styles are created by mapping standard ASCII characters to universal mathematical, phonetic, and typographic symbols already built into the global Unicode standard (UTF-8).

This means you can copy and paste these stylized characters anywhere: Instagram bios, Discord usernames, Reddit comments, Twitter/X tweets, TikTok captions, and YouTube comments without losing their formatting!
