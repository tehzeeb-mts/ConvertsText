The Builder Story

Why I built ConvertsText

Tired of slow, ad-choked converter websites that upload your text to unknown servers? So was I.

Built for speed, simplicity, and total privacy.

Hey there! I'm Tehzeeb, the independent developer behind ConvertsText.

Like many developers and writers, I found myself constantly converting text: turning raw copy into AP Title Case for headlines, transforming variable strings into camelCase or snake_case, or stripping formatting from messy PDF snippets. But whenever I searched for an online case converter, I was greeted by the same frustrating experience: sluggish page loads, deceptive "Download" ads, aggressive popup banners, and questionable privacy policies that sent my text to remote servers.

I built ConvertsText to be the tool I always wanted to use: lightweight, beautifully designed, keyboard-friendly, and completely private.

Zero Server Uploads

Everything runs strictly in your browser's local memory using pure JavaScript. Your private notes, code, and customer data never touch a backend server.

Instant Keyboard Flow

No page refreshes or spinning loaders. Press Ctrl+K to jump between 40+ tools instantly, or use Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y to undo and redo transforms.

Accurate Editorial Logic

Our Title Case engine follows AP and Chicago editorial rules, keeping minor conjunctions and prepositions lowercase so you don't have to manually fix them.

How ConvertsText Stays Free

ConvertsText is funded by clean, non-intrusive advertisements and optional affiliate recommendations for trusted writing software. We never sell user data, lock core casing tools behind paywalls, or require account creation.

Questions or Suggestions?

I actively maintain and improve ConvertsText based on real user feedback. If you have a suggestion for a new casing algorithm, spotted an edge-case formatting bug, or just want to say hi, please reach out through the contact page.
