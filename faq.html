Standard Case Conversions & Text Formatting

4 ENTRIES
CASING What is an online case converter tool and how does it work?
▾

An online case converter is a web-based text utility that algorithmically alters the capitalization of letters in your text. Instead of manually retyping sentences or code identifiers, you can convert text instantly between formats like Sentence case, UPPERCASE, lowercase, Title Case, Capitalized Case, and aLtErNaTiNg cAsE with a single click.

CASING How do I fix text accidentally typed in ALL CAPS with Caps Lock on?
▾

Paste your text into the ConvertsText editor and click the Sentence case button. The algorithm automatically lowers all unnecessary capitals and capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence, as well as standalone personal pronouns like "I". You can also use lower case to make every character lowercase.

STYLE What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalized Case?
▾

Capitalized Case blindly capitalizes the first letter of every single word (e.g., "The Old Man And The Sea"). In contrast, Title Case follows editorial style rules (such as Chicago, APA, and MLA style) by keeping short grammatical minor words lowercase (e.g., "a", "an", "the", "and", "but", "for", "in", "of", "to") unless they are the first or last word of the title (e.g., "The Old Man and the Sea").

INTERNET What is alternating case (mOcKiNg cAsE) and where is it used?
▾

Alternating case toggles between uppercase and lowercase characters (e.g., "yOu CaNnOt Do ThAt"). It became widely popularized on social media and internet culture through the SpongeBob Mocking meme to convey sarcasm, satire, or irony in digital communication.

// 02_DEVELOPER_IDENTIFIERS_&_NAMING_CONVENTIONS

Developer Identifier Conventions (camelCase, snake_case, kebab-case)

4 ENTRIES
DEV_SPEC What is the difference between camelCase and PascalCase?
▾

Both join words without spaces, capitalizing the first letter of each subsequent word. However, camelCase begins with a lowercase letter (e.g., calculateTaxRate), standard for variables and functions in JavaScript, Java, and TypeScript. PascalCase begins with a capital letter (e.g., CalculateTaxRate), standard for classes, components (React), and types.

DEV_SPEC When should I use snake_case vs kebab-case vs CONSTANT_CASE?
▾

snake_case (e.g. user_profile_id) is standard in Python, Ruby, PostgreSQL, and SQL column naming. kebab-case (e.g. user-profile-id) is standard for SEO URL slugs, HTML attributes, and CSS class names because search engines treat hyphens as word delimiters. CONSTANT_CASE (e.g. MAX_UPLOAD_SIZE) is used across all languages for immutable global constants and environment variables.

DEV_SPEC What are dot.case and path/case used for?
▾

dot.case (e.g. app.config.max.connections) is standard for configuration files, properties, and microservice keys. path/case (e.g. src/components/user/profile) is used for file system paths and modular imports.

PARSER Can ConvertsText decompose camelCase back into readable words with spaces?
▾

Yes. ConvertsText uses a smart lossless tokenization engine. If you paste an identifier like calculateMonthlyAmortizationPayment or USER_ACCOUNT_ID, converting it to Title Case or Sentence case automatically unpacks the words into "Calculate Monthly Amortization Payment" with proper spacing.

// 04_READABILITY_FORMULAS_&_METRICS

Readability Scores, Syllable Counts & Word Metrics

3 ENTRIES
METRIC How is the Flesch Reading Ease score calculated and what does it mean?
▾

The Flesch Reading Ease score ranges from 0 to 100:

  • 90–100: Very Easy (5th-grade level, easily understood by an 11-year-old).
  • 60–70: Standard / Plain English (8th–9th grade level, ideal for web copy, blogs, and marketing).
  • 0–30: Very Difficult (College graduate level, academic journals, and legal documents).
METRIC What is the Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level formula?
▾

The Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level translates reading difficulty directly into US school grade levels using the formula: 0.39 * (Total Words / Total Sentences) + 11.8 * (Total Syllables / Total Words) - 15.59. A score of 8.0 indicates an 8th-grade reading standard.

SPEED How are Reading Time and Speaking Time estimated?
▾

Reading time is calculated using the global standard adult silent reading rate of 225 words per minute (WPM). Speaking time is calculated based on the average conversational presentation pace of 130 words per minute (WPM).
